# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

SlopGuard adapts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), under its CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

## Our Pledge

We want participation in the SlopGuard community to be respectful and harassment-free. We welcome people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability. We commit to listening, sharing constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and kindness.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Apologizing when our actions harm others and learning from the experience.
- Considering what benefits the whole community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal attacks, harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. Representation includes using an official project email address, posting from an official account, or acting as an appointed project representative.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They may edit or remove contributions that do not meet the code and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Maintainers will take fair corrective action and protect the privacy of people who report an incident.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [ahmadchaudhary722@gmail.com](mailto:ahmadchaudhary722@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers use these response guidelines:

1. **Correction:** For unprofessional or unwelcome behavior, a maintainer may send a private written correction and request a public apology.
2. **Warning:** For a single or repeated violation, a maintainer may issue a warning and limit contact with affected people or enforcement volunteers for a stated period.
3. **Temporary ban:** For serious or sustained violations, a maintainer may temporarily restrict participation in project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban:** For a repeated pattern of serious violations, a maintainer may permanently restrict participation in project spaces.
